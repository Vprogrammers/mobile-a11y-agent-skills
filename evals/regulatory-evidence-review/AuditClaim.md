# Release statement

Our automated Android and iOS accessibility audits are green. Therefore the mobile app is WCAG 2.2, EAA, ADA, Section 508, and EN 301 549 compliant in every market. Because EN 301 549 V4.1.1 is the newest published edition, it is automatically harmonized and creates a presumption of conformity across the EU. Manual assistive-technology testing is unnecessary. The same report applies to future releases.
