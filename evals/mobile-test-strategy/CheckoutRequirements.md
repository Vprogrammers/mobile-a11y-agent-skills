# Checkout requirements

- Shared KMP validation rejects an empty cardholder name.
- Compose and SwiftUI expose the validation error.
- A custom amount slider supports equivalent increment/decrement actions.
- The confirmation overlay traps focus and restores it when dismissed.
- Payment details must not be copied into screenshots, logs, or test artifacts.
- Product asks CI to prove the exact TalkBack and VoiceOver announcement text.
