# United States accessibility statement

The app passed an automated WCAG 2.2 scan, so it complies with ADA Title II, ADA Title III, and Section 508 throughout the United States. These laws all require WCAG 2.2 for every native mobile application. Section 508 does not require any software interoperability or functional-performance review beyond WCAG. The same scan proves that third-party SDK screens and future releases are compliant.
