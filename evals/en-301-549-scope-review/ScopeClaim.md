# Accessibility scope statement

The product is a native Android and iOS shopping app. Checkout is implemented with native Compose and SwiftUI screens, card payment opens an embedded WebView, and the receipt is downloadable as a tagged PDF.

Because the product runs on phones, every surface is web content and only EN 301 549 clause 9 needs evaluation. EN 301 549 V4.1.1 is the latest publication, so it is automatically the legally applicable edition in every EU country. One successful automated scan of the checkout screen proves conformity for the complete release.
